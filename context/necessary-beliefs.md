# Necessary Beliefs — {{BRAND_NAME}} {{Hero Product}}

*The minimum sequence of beliefs a prospect must hold, in order, before they will purchase. Each belief is load-bearing for the one that follows — skipping or reordering any of them breaks the argument. Every ad concept should advance at least one of these.*

> 💡 Usually 4–7 beliefs. Write each in the prospect's voice, as "I believe that…". A typical arc runs from the problem being real, to the category being right for me, to the category's fears being myths, to why I failed before, to this specific product being trustworthy, to me actually seeing results. `/onboard` can draft this from your offer brief and customer research.
